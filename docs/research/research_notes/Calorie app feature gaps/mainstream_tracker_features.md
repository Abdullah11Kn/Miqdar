# Mainstream calorie and nutrition tracker features (2025-2026), for comparison with Miqdar

Researched 2026-11-14. Source quality note: official pricing pages for MyFitnessPal, Yazio and Lose It could not be fetched (403/404 or timeouts). Where possible, App Store listings and vendor pages (primary) are used; otherwise third-party review sites. Many "review" sites in this space (nutriscan.app, nutrola.app, fitbudd, trygaya, mycallie, etc.) are run by competing apps and are marked as such. Prices are US unless stated and vary by region, platform and promotion.

## 1. Core and premium features per app (free vs paid)

### Takeaway
Every app now offers AI photo logging and most offer voice/text logging, nearly always behind the paywall. Free tiers keep manual search logging, basic calorie goals and (usually) water tracking; barcode scanning is paid at MyFitnessPal and Lose It but free at Cronometer and (apparently) Yazio. Cronometer's free tier is the most generous (95 nutrients, barcode, integrations).

### Inferences
- The free/paid line has converged: **AI logging (photo, voice) is the main paywall lever across the industry.** Miqdar gives all of this free (subject to the Gemini free quota).
- Copy previous day, saved meals and recent foods are table stakes; MFP and Cronometer ("Repeat Items") both have them. Miqdar has quick re-log of recent foods but no evidence of "copy yesterday" or saved/favourite meals as a named feature.

### Gaps
- Official MFP, Yazio and Lose It pricing pages could not be fetched; tier-to-price mapping is from third-party sources.
- No reliable source found on widgets and Apple Watch apps per app in 2026 (the App Store pages fetched did not describe them, apart from MacroFactor listing watchOS 11 compatibility and MFP listing Wear OS).
- Restaurant menu databases (a historic MFP feature) were not confirmed in any 2025-2026 source.
- Water tracking: confirmed for MFP, Yazio, Lifesum, Fitbit; not confirmed for Cronometer, MacroFactor, Noom, Lose It in fetched sources.

## 3. What makes MacroFactor and Cronometer stand out

### Takeaway
MacroFactor's edge is its adaptive expenditure algorithm (TDEE learned from intake and weight trend, targets updated weekly) plus a verified database and fast logging. Cronometer's edge is data quality: lab-analysed/verified foods and up to 95 nutrients tracked free, with clinician-oriented tools (PDF reports, custom biometrics, charts).

### Cited Findings
- MacroFactor estimates expenditure from food logs and weight data and adjusts calorie and macro targets at weekly check-ins; Weight Trend smooths daily fluctuation; Coached / Collaborative / Manual modes — [App Store, MacroFactor](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/macrofactor-macro-tracker/id1553503471)
- The estimate starts from a generic equation and converges on real expenditure after about 2 to 4 weeks of consistent logging and weighing (third-party) — [Amy Food Journal](https://www.amyfoodjournal.com/blog/macrofactor-review)
- MacroFactor has an optional "expenditure modifier" adding step-informed data and goal-based adjustments; Oct 2026 digest on expenditure accuracy — [MacroFactor](https://macrofactor.com/)
- MacroFactor's AI logging splits a meal into ingredients via several LLM prompts, then queries its database for real foods instead of letting the LLM invent entries; prioritises "real, lab-analyzed results"; warns AI can miss hidden sauces and misjudge portions; users should review every result — [MacroFactor, AI food logging (Apr 2025)](https://macrofactor.com/ai-food-logging/)
- July 2026: label scanner reads nutrition labels across formats and languages, using front-of-pack plus label photos to create a food — [MacroFactor July 2026 digest](https://macrofactor.com/mm-july-2026/)
- Cronometer: database of over 1M lab-analysed or verified foods, up to 95 nutrients free, custom micronutrient targets — [Cronometer Gold](https://cronometer.com/gold)
- Cronometer is repeatedly named the best free calorie counter / most accurate for micronutrients in roundups — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/best-calorie-counter-apps/); [Welling](https://www.welling.ai/articles/best-calorie-counting-apps-which-one-is-right-for-you)

### Inferences
- **Most relevant gap for Miqdar:** Miqdar's goals come from a fixed Mifflin-St Jeor equation. It already has a body weight log and an accurate weighed-intake diary, which are exactly the two inputs MacroFactor-style adaptive TDEE needs. A weighed scale makes intake data more accurate than typical app logging, so adaptive expenditure would be unusually strong here.
- MacroFactor's "ground AI output in a database" design is the opposite of Miqdar's decision (Gemini analyses every food, no database lookup). Miqdar's per-gram weighing removes the biggest AI error (portion size), which MacroFactor's own page lists as a weakness of photo AI.
- Cronometer's micronutrient depth (95 nutrients) far exceeds Miqdar's set (macros, sugar, free sugar, fiber, sodium, potassium).

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy study of MacroFactor's algorithm or Cronometer's database was found in this pass.
- MacroFactor's "adherence-neutral" design philosophy (no shaming, no streak pressure) is widely cited but was not confirmed in a fetched source.

## 4. New features launched 2024-2026

### Takeaway
2025-2026 was the year of AI logging (photo, voice, text description, label reading) and AI coaches, plus GLP-1 support. MFP bought Cal AI; Fitbit rebuilt around Gemini; MacroFactor, Cronometer, Yazio, Lifesum and Lose It all added photo/voice logging.

### Cited Findings
- MFP acquired Cal AI (photo-calorie app), announced mid-March 2026; standalone app to be wound down and its technology folded into MFP Premium; price undisclosed — [The Nutrition Magazine](https://thenutritionmagazine.com/articles/myfitnesspal-acquires-cal-ai/) (article dates inconsistent; no primary source linked)
- May 2026 MFP Premium expansion reportedly added scan-a-meal, recipe URL import and macro-by-meal goals (secondary) — [The Nutrition Magazine](https://thenutritionmagazine.com/articles/myfitnesspal-acquires-cal-ai/)
- MFP: GLP-1 medication logging with reminders; AI Coach for Premium — [App Store, MFP](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/myfitnesspal-calorie-counter/id341232718)
- MacroFactor AI photo logging beta, April 2025 — [MacroFactor](https://macrofactor.com/ai-food-logging/); recent versions add AI custom food creation and label scanning (5.8.3) and AI photo/text recipe import (5.7.6) — [App Store, MacroFactor](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/macrofactor-macro-tracker/id1553503471)
- Cronometer Gold now lists Photo Log, Voice Log (incl. Siri), Crono Coach AI and a ChatGPT/Codex plugin — [Cronometer Gold](https://cronometer.com/gold)
- Fitbit Gemini Coach preview Oct 2025; nutrition added Mar-Apr 2026; app renamed Google Health by May 2026 with food-tracker backlash — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/27/fitbits-revamped-app-with-gemini-powered-health-coach-rolls-out-to-premium-users/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/03/31/fitbit-app-redesign-free/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/27/google-health-roadmap-fitbit-backlash/)
- Yazio AI photo logging late 2025, for PRO (competitor source) — [NutriScan](https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/yazio-pricing-2026-free-vs-pro-what-pro-unlocks-33b26f8fc7)
- Lifesum multimodal AI tracking (photo, voice, barcode, text) — [lifesumai.com](https://lifesumai.com/); launch date of Feb 2025 claimed only by a comparison site, unconfirmed.
- Lose It: photo meal logging and AI voice logging now Premium — [App Store, Lose It!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lose-it-calorie-counter/id297368629)
- Noom: AI food logging, body and face scans, Noom Med GLP-1 programmes including microdose — [App Store, Noom](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/noom-weight-loss-food-tracker/id634598719)

### Inferences
- Feature comparison against Miqdar, as a checklist (from the findings above):
  - **Miqdar already matches or beats:** AI photo + text + voice logging (free); mixed plates; estimates labelled; macros + sugar/fiber/sodium/potassium; body-profile goals; weight log; reminders; re-log recent; clinician PDF report (only Cronometer Gold has this); diabetes mode (Lose It tracks glucose; MFP has Net Carbs; none found with a carb-per-meal plan); Ramadan mode (none found); leftovers weighing (none found; weighed intake is unique to a scale); Arabic.
  - **Common features Miqdar lacks:** barcode scanning; label scanning; adaptive TDEE; Apple Health / Google Fit / wearable sync (steps, exercise calories); water tracking; intermittent fasting timer; saved meals / copy previous day; recipe builder and URL import; meal plans and grocery lists; macro scheduling by weekday; deeper micronutrients; long-range custom charts; widgets / Watch; community and gamification (Lose It, Noom); accounts and cloud backup; GLP-1 medication logging (MFP, Noom).
- Industry is moving toward grounding AI in verified databases (MacroFactor, MFP via Cal AI). Miqdar's grams-from-the-scale is a different route to the same accuracy goal and a defensible differentiator.

### Gaps
- No primary press release for the MFP/Cal AI deal was fetched; date and terms are from secondary sources.
- Exact launch dates for Lose It Snap It voice, Lifesum AI and Yazio AI photo are not confirmed by primary sources.
- No mainstream app was found with Arabic-first or Gulf-food-specific databases, but this was not searched explicitly.
